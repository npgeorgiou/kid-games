# Proposals: theme and mechanics

Every proposal in `docs/brief.txt` split into theme and mechanics. Mechanics here are a **brainstorm**, not decisions; nothing is built until it's on an approved design sheet. A game = mechanics + theme, so any theme in a row can be put on another row's mechanics.

## What Tower Builder taught (brief → final)

| Brief said | Final game did | Rule for the next games |
|---|---|---|
| "Receive a construction brief", reach a height line | Each level is one real famous building, shown as a faint outline to fill | Levels come from real things, not abstract goals. The real thing is the level design. |
| "Stability test" | Big button → wind, then the building's own load is lowered on, then an earthquake | Plan → press one button → watch a staged show. Results are a show, not a number. |
| Generic sites (quarry, acropolis) | Ten monuments in chronological order on a world map, each adding one demand | Progression = a real timeline or map; each level adds exactly one new idea. |
| "Show the weak point" | Cut: the fall is the explanation | Cause and effect shown in the world, not by UI markers. |
| Optional goals | Symbols, no numbers | No numbers a 4-year-old must read. |
| (nothing) | Free build with no goal; knocking down is fun | Every game needs a toy mode that's fun with no goal. |
| "Wobble indication" | Every level must have wrong choices (including wrong material) that visibly fail | Each level needs tempting wrong choices that visibly fail. |
| (nothing) | Finished monument stays on the map | Progress you can see. |
| Hit the height | Forgiving fill check | Lenient success checks; strict physics. |

Recurring template: **toy → plan → commit → watch the show → see why → retry**, levels taken from real Greek things, one new idea per level, visible progress.

## Per proposal

Columns: **Theme** (from brief) · **System** (standard game type it is) · **Brainstormed mechanics** (in the template above) · **Theme swaps** (alternative themes for the same mechanics; ♀ = female hero or care/rescue/creative theme).

### 2. Donkey Caravan Trader
- Theme: merchant caravan, ancient/Hellenistic mainland and island towns.
- System: trade arbitrage (Patrician, Taipan), scaled down.
- Mechanics: map of real towns, each shown by a picture of what it makes and what it wants (no prices). Load donkey baskets by dragging goods; weight slows the donkey and too much makes it stop. Press go → the trip plays out (mountain pass, river ford). Coins are physical tokens piled up and spent on a second donkey or a cart. Wrong choice: carrying olive oil to a town full of olives gives no coins.
- Swaps: ♀ a girl merchant; a honey/beekeeping route; a modern island bakery van.

### 3. Castle Conquest
- Theme: Byzantine forts on a strategic map.
- System: send-troops RTS (Galcon, State.io).
- Mechanics: forts fill up with little figures over time; drag from your fort to another to send half; same-sized groups cancel out. Neutral forts shown as grey. Levels are real Byzantine fortress sites. Wrong choice: emptying a fort that's next to an enemy one.
- Swaps: ♀ shepherdess moving flocks between mountain pastures (wolves take empty pastures); bees and hives; Pindus villages sharing bread.

### 4. Women of Pindus
- Theme: 1940–41, women carrying supplies over the Pindus.
- System: side-scrolling platformer with load weight (Death Stranding idea, kid-scaled).
- Mechanics: choose how many sacks to load before setting off; each sack lowers the jump and slows climbing. Path forks: short and steep vs long and gentle. Snow, mud, river stones, a mule to borrow. Deliver → the post fills up visibly. Wrong choice: too heavy for a jump gap means unloading or a detour.
- Swaps: ♀ already. Could become a mountain postwoman or a goatherd bringing milk to the village.

### 5. Factory Automation
- Theme: modern Greek food plant.
- System: conveyor logistics (Factorio, Shapez), scaled down.
- Mechanics: grid; drag belts and machines (wash, press, bottle); raw items enter, products must reach the truck. Press run → watch items flow; jams pile up visibly where they happen. Levels are real Greek products: olive oil, feta, honey, oranges to juice.
- Swaps: ♀ a sweets/loukoumi kitchen; a chocolate line; a perfume or soap workshop.

### 6. Bridge Builder
- Theme: ancient/Byzantine bridges over ravines and rivers.
- System: Poly Bridge / Bridge Constructor. **Shares the Tower Builder physics and test.**
- Mechanics: real bridges as outlines (e.g. Arkadiko Mycenaean bridge, Ottoman-era stone bridges of Epirus). Build, pull out the wooden centring, then a cart, a donkey, a procession crosses. Wrong choice: a wooden beam too long, an arch missing its keystone.
- Swaps: ♀ building a bridge so a goat herd or a school group can cross.

### 7. Little Fighters
- Theme: ancient athletic festival duel.
- System: two-button reaction duel (Punch-Out!!).
- Mechanics: each opponent has a readable tell (raises arm = about to strike high); punch or kick at the right gap; dodging is automatic if you don't attack too early. Opponents are a series with distinct patterns; a wreath for each win.
- Swaps: ♀ dance-off (copy-and-answer timing); ball games; a girls' race at Heraia (an ancient festival for women at Olympia). Same tell-and-answer timing.

### 8. Airport Controller
- Theme: modern Greek island airport.
- System: line-drawing traffic (Flight Control).
- Mechanics: draw a path with your finger from plane to runway; planes follow it; two paths that meet too close flash and a plane circles. Colour-coded runways. Real airports as levels (small island strips first).
- Swaps: ♀ seaplanes; hot-air balloons; storks landing on nests.

### 9. Village Builder
- Theme: ancient/Hellenistic/Byzantine settlement.
- System: compact city builder (Townscaper + Islanders).
- Mechanics: drag buildings onto a small map; each needs things nearby (house near a well, oven near the field); paths connect themselves. Villagers walk the paths and show what they lack with a picture bubble. Levels are real terrain types: island slope, river plain, mountain.
- Swaps: ♀ a nest or garden builder; an island cat colony.

### 10. Cargo Train Routing
- Theme: modern Greek railway.
- System: track puzzle (Mini Metro, Train Valley).
- Mechanics: place track tiles; tap switches to flip them; coloured trains must reach the stations of the same colour. Press go → trains run; collision is shown as a gentle crash and reset.
- Swaps: ♀ Pelion mountain toy train; a funicular; a toy-train playroom.

### 11. Castle Defense
- Theme: Byzantine walled town.
- System: tower defense (Kingdom Rush), slowed down.
- Mechanics: fixed paths; place defenders on marked spots before each wave; a wave plays without input. Abstract invaders (the brief says abstract). Wrong choice: all defenders in one place.
- Swaps: ♀ a garden protected from snails and crows (scarecrows, fences); a beehive against wasps.

### 12. Mediterranean Merchant Shipping
- Theme: ancient sea trade.
- System: trade arbitrage plus sailing navigation. **Overlaps #2**; the difference is wind and sea.
- Mechanics: same port-needs pictures as #2; plus wind direction arrows on the map, and you choose the route around islands; storms and calm. Ship capacity by hull spaces.
- Swaps: ♀ Bouboulina's ship (1821); a fishing boat; a ferry bringing visitors.

### 13. Island Conquest
- Theme: Hellenistic islands and harbours.
- System: send-troops with supply lines. **Overlaps #3.**
- Mechanics: like #3 but ships travel along sea lanes and an island cut off from its harbour stops producing.
- Swaps: ♀ lighthouse-keepers lighting islands; spreading flowers between islands.

### 14. Phalanx Formation Tactics
- Theme: classical hoplites.
- System: formation tactics (simplified Total War).
- Mechanics: drag to shape the block and turn its facing; the field has hills and rivers; enemy blocks move on a fixed pattern. A block is strong in front, weak at the sides. Press go → both move.
- Swaps: ♀ a dance troupe in formation; herding geese; a choir procession.

### 15. Farm Manager
- Theme: Hellenistic/Byzantine farm.
- System: seasonal farm sim (Stardew lite, Harvest Moon).
- Mechanics: few fields; choose a crop by season (wheat, vines, olives); seasons advance by button; animals need feeding; take produce to the market. Wrong choice: planting at the wrong season, nothing grows.
- Swaps: ♀ already fits; a beekeeper; a flower farm.

### 16. Zoo Manager
- Theme: modern Greek wildlife rescue park.
- System: habitat builder (Planet Zoo lite).
- Mechanics: a rescued animal arrives; build its enclosure from parts (water, shade, rocks, plants, food); the animal shows by behaviour whether it's happy. Release it to the wild when healed. Real Greek animals: loggerhead turtle, monk seal, brown bear, griffon vulture.
- Swaps: ♀ already fits; a vet clinic.

### 17. Restaurant Manager
- Theme: modern taverna kitchen.
- System: order-sequencing (Overcooked, non-frantic).
- Mechanics: an order shows the dish as a picture; drag ingredients through stations in the right order (cut → grill → plate); orders wait patiently, no timers at first. Real Greek dishes as levels.
- Swaps: ♀ bakery (koulourakia, Easter bread); an ice-cream shop.

### 18. Mechanical Workshop
- Theme: Hellenistic engineering workshop.
- System: contraption puzzle (Incredible Machine). **Shares physics with #1.**
- Mechanics: place gears, levers, pulleys, ropes; press crank → watch motion pass through. Real inventions as levels (Heron's door, Archimedes screw, a crane).
- Swaps: ♀ a toy-maker's workshop; music box.

### 19. Waterworks
- Theme: ancient water engineering.
- System: flow puzzle (Where's My Water, pipe games).
- Mechanics: dig or place channels; gates open/close; water flows by gravity in real time; flooding is visible. Real sites: Eupalinos tunnel (Samos), Byzantine cisterns.
- Swaps: ♀ watering a garden; filling a fountain; a duck pond.

### 20. Siege Engineer
- Theme: catapult practice in a training ground.
- System: projectile physics (Angry Birds, Crush the Castle).
- Mechanics: pull back to set force, tilt for angle; weight of the stone changes the arc; the last shot's arc stays drawn. Targets: baskets, wooden towers.
- Swaps: ♀ throwing seeds to birds; a fruit-catching game; water balloons at a summer fair.

### 21. Port Manager
- Theme: modern Greek port.
- System: dock logistics (Port Royale lite). **Overlaps #5, #38.**
- Mechanics: ships come with coloured containers; assign a berth; crane moves boxes to trucks of the same colour; berths fill up.
- Swaps: ♀ a fishing harbour; a ferry with passengers and luggage.

### 22. Rescue Helicopter
- Theme: modern civil protection.
- System: thrust-control flyer (Lunar Lander, Choplifter).
- Mechanics: hold to rise, tilt to move; land softly on a pad; winch down to pick up; fuel shown as a picture bar. Real Greek terrain: Samaria gorge, Meteora, an island cliff.
- Swaps: ♀ rescue pilot; flying vet; delivering presents to islands.

### 23. Fire Department
- Theme: modern Greek fire service.
- System: resource dispatch + aiming (Firefighters lite).
- Mechanics: choose a route on a small town map; park near a hydrant; drag the hose; aim the water stream; fire spreads slowly with the wind so priorities matter.
- Swaps: ♀ firefighter; a forest watch with a water plane.

### 24. Road Network Builder
- Theme: modern Greek roads.
- System: network layout (Mini Motorways).
- Mechanics: draw roads between houses and destinations of the same colour; limited road pieces; cars show where it's congested.
- Swaps: ♀ paths for a school walk; ant trails.

### 25. Traffic Controller
- Theme: modern town intersections.
- System: signal timing puzzle.
- Mechanics: tap a light to switch it; cars wait and queue; a full queue blocks the other junction. Few cars at first.
- Swaps: ♀ a lollipop lady crossing schoolchildren; ducklings crossing.

### 26. Treasure Island Exploration
- Theme: Hellenistic island, sanctuary, caves.
- System: point-and-click adventure (Machinarium-style, no text).
- Mechanics: tap to walk; tools found are used where they fit (rope at a well, key at a door); a map fills in as you explore.
- Swaps: ♀ girl explorer; a lost cat to find.

### 27. Lost Mine
- Theme: Byzantine-era mine/quarry.
- System: exploration + mechanism puzzle. **Overlaps #18, #26.**
- Mechanics: carts on rails, lifts, counterweights; restore each machine to open the next area.
- Swaps: ♀ a crystal cave; a mole's tunnels.

### 28. Adventure Platformer
- Theme: myth-inspired Greece, a young messenger.
- System: Metroidvania-lite platformer.
- Mechanics: each region gives one new move (wind sandals, a lyre that moves stones); switches, secrets; real landscapes per region (Olympus, Delphi, Crete).
- Swaps: ♀ a young messenger girl; Iris the rainbow messenger.

### 29. Beat-'em-up Adventure
- Theme: myth festival, comic troublemakers.
- System: side-scrolling brawler (Castle Crashers lite). **Overlaps #7, #28.**
- Mechanics: walk right; push or dodge troublemakers; throw objects; a few moves.
- Swaps: ♀ pillow-fight festival; shooing goats out of a garden.

### 30. Fish Evolution
- Theme: Aegean sea life.
- System: grow-and-survive (feeding frenzy, Spore cell stage).
- Mechanics: steer a fish; eat the right food; hide from bigger fish in seagrass; growing opens new areas. Real Aegean species.
- Swaps: ♀ a baby turtle from beach to sea; an octopus.

### 31. Pinball
- Theme: modern arcade with Greek-themed tables.
- System: pinball.
- Mechanics: two flippers by tapping left/right; each table has a mission shown by lights (light the temple's columns).
- Swaps: ♀ a garden or mosaic table.

### 32. Racing
- Theme: modern Greek roads and islands.
- System: kart racer, assisted steering.
- Mechanics: choose lane/line at forks rather than fine steering; surfaces slow you; shortcuts.
- Swaps: ♀ bicycle race; donkey race; sailing regatta.

### 33. Submarine Exploration
- Theme: modern Aegean research.
- System: exploration with energy limit (Subnautica lite, 2D).
- Mechanics: steer; sonar ping shows nearby objects; battery as a picture; return before it empties. Real finds: shipwrecks, Antikythera site.
- Swaps: ♀ marine biologist; a dolphin.

### 34. Bubble Shooter
- Theme: seaside festival / mosaic fantasy.
- System: bubble shooter.
- Mechanics: aim and shoot; groups of three fall; walls bounce; level goal is freeing an object.
- Swaps: ♀ fits as is.

### 35. Match-3
- Theme: mosaics, pottery, fruit.

- Mechanics: swap neighbours; level goals (clear the tiles over a mosaic to reveal it).
- Swaps: ♀ fits as is.

### 36. Robot Programming
- Theme: modern science museum.
- System: command-sequence puzzle (Lightbot, Robot Turtles).
- Mechanics: drag arrow tiles into a row; press play; the robot walks the program; it stops where the program goes wrong.
- Swaps: ♀ programming a cat or a dancer; Heron-style automaton (Hellenistic theatre automata).

### 37. Construction Site and Cranes
- Theme: modern construction site.
- System: crane control physics. **Shares physics with #1.**
- Mechanics: drag to move the hook, hold to lower; loads swing; place on marks without hitting things.
- Swaps: ♀ crane-girl; an ancient wooden crane (a Tower Builder level).

### 38. Airport Manager
- Theme: modern airport ground ops.

- Swaps: ♀ preparing a ferry; a school bus trip.

### 39. Treasure Hunt — Clue and Deduction

- Swaps: ♀ girl detective; a missing pet.
