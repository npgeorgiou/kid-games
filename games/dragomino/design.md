## Rejected engines

- Each land pays its size times the symbols in it (boat on sea, press on olive). Rejected: makes lands differ in value.
- Each land pays 1 coin, whatever its size. Rejected: still makes "many lands" worth more; every land is the same value.
- Each square makes 1 good of its terrain (fish, bread, oil, wine, stone); 3 different goods buy first pick or a second tile. Rejected by the owner's questions: ties when both players have a set, jars have no job beside sets, and with about 20 tiles each sets pile up.

## Open questions

- If every square is worth the same and everyone places one tile per turn, every player's land produces the same, so land cannot be an engine. Lands must differ by how they are built. Two options keeping all terrains equal:
  - Shape: a bigger connected land is worth more than the same squares split up (like Kingdomino's area scoring, without symbols).
  - Luck: jars decide the differences (like Dragomino).
