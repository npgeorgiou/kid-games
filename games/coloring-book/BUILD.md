# Coloring Book

## Now
Sandbox canvas, paint engine on the graphics card (rebuilt 21 Nov), aiming at Rebelle's look; owner's verdict open. Media: oil, acrylic (dries fast, thinner, little gloss), watercolour (flows, bleeds while wet, darker rim, dries in about 10 s), crayon (wax on the canvas grain, streaky, pressing harder fills more), simple brush (softness). Each medium has 3 brush shapes (oil and acrylic: round, flat, filbert; watercolour: round, flat, mop; crayon: point, blunt, side; simple: round, flat, square) and 5 sizes. Holding a colour longer takes more paint (1-3 dots, one per half second); a brush low on paint touches only the raised grain. Pen pressure, or finger contact size on touch screens. Wet paints mix with Mixbox (lib/mixbox, CC BY-NC). Canvases: watercolour paper, smooth paper, cotton canvas, linen (canvas button: a new page on it). Palettes: 4 sets of 11 colours. Guide picture: see-through on top, tap to show/hide, hold to pick another. Undo (button and Ctrl+Z), new, gallery, delete (press twice within 6 s, as the architects game's restart). Icons: Phosphor (lib/phosphor, MIT). Open: everything in the sheet's "Prototype must answer" row; drying times; outline pictures to colour in come later.
