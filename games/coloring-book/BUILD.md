Tools: oil, acrylic (dries in about 15 s), watercolour (flows, bleeds while wet, darker rim, dries in about 10 s), crayon (wax on the paper's fine tooth, streaks along the stroke, more passes fill more), marker (see-through ink, crossing lines darker), simple (plain soft dot icon, softness).
