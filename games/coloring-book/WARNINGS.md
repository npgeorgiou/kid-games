# Coloring Book: warnings

- Oil rates are per pixel of finger travel (times the dab spacing), never per dab, or the brush runs dry at a different length for every size.
- Oil looks like pinstripes or corrugated tubes when bristle strength changes pixel to pixel or shadows go dark; streaks must be soft (blurred bristles) and lit mostly by highlights, checked on a full-resolution crop, not a scaled screenshot.
- Watercolour pigment moves by flow and by diffusion in the same step; their combined outflow must stay under what a pixel holds (diffusion capped at 0.1 per neighbour), or clamping negatives creates pigment and the picture turns black.
- Water must never make paper damp in proportion to a thin film while thin films can also push onto dry paper: the wet area then creeps without end and evaporates in seconds.
- The headless test browser draws with the processor at about 8 frames a second, so wet paint runs in slow motion there; judge the look with __cb.fast(seconds), and give watercolour tests long waits.
- Never mix paint with the paper's white through Mixbox: Mixbox treats white as a pigment, so thin paint turns pastel (red goes pink). Paint over paper or dry paint uses Kubelka-Munk; Mixbox only between wet paints.
- Brush rates per dab must use 1 - exp(-rate): with the big brush sizes a linear rate exceeds 1 and takes more paint than there is.
- A tap on a colour gives a full brush; holding goes round a third, two thirds, full (0.5 s steps); hold(q, 'color-N', 1.6) is also full. Sizes, brushes and softness are in popups: the flows' tap() opens the popup first.
- Undo of old steps is asynchronous (they come back from compressed memory); undo presses are queued, never dropped. Waking the water simulation after an undo only when that step had wet paint, or undo crawls.
- Acrylic and the oil pastel (crayon) keep the paint's own colour (PIC) and what is under it (UNDER, with the coat's thickness in its alpha); everything else (simple, marker, watercolour) must write the same colour into both, or the coat shows old colours.
- The undo store and its spill to memory cover every state texture (STATES); adding a picture texture means adding it there.
- Coolors pages are drawn by script: read them through the cloud browser's page snapshot, not WebFetch.
- Never put a // comment in the middle of a one-line object literal when patching: it silently comments out the rest of the line (a stroke lost its colour and nothing painted).
- A picture's palette from plain cluster averages comes out beige and misses small bright parts: use the more colourful half of each group and weigh colourfulness.

- Brush popup marks are real strokes painted by tools/previews.py (lib/previews, under 2 minutes): rerun it after any change to how a tool, brush or canvas looks, or the popup shows the old look.
- Wet paint is worked only on runs of wet tiles (waterRuns), never the box around all wet places; any new water pass goes over those runs, and the flow is computed one pixel past each run.
- Only number entries of K become shader constants; lists (like CRAY_PRESS) stay in the script.
- Marker and simple lay one coverage per stroke over the colour from before the stroke (MISC g/b, B0). The crayon is an oil pastel on the acrylic brush model (MED.crayon, REACH for the grain); the OIL_ constants are the pastel's (no oil tool). Look at crayon strokes at full size before sending: numbers passed while it painted fish scales.
- Flows pick colours by their place in the Basic palette (6 is blue); if the Basic palette changes, change the places in the flows, never what they check.
- The undo room spills old steps to memory ahead of time while nobody paints (keepRoom); a step read back while he paints made strokes lag and show late.
- Never put a // comment inside a GLSL template line either: it comments out the rest of the shader line and the page never becomes ready.
- Acrylic bristles are new for every stroke (random), so a streak can sit on any one pixel: check a brush stroke's colour across it (across()), never on one pixel.
- A test paper must differ from the full paper only in size: its canvas grain is the full paper's grain at that place (paperGrain). If a flow's numbers change on its test paper, run it on the full paper to compare.
- The test browser stalls each mouse move while heavy paint is drawn: drags put the pointer where the finger would be by now (drag); only frame-time flows send one fixed move after another (per_move).
- The commit gate runs the old code outside the repo and at the same time as the new: harnesses and flows use only their own folder and tempfile.gettempdir(), never fixed /tmp paths.
- The test browser sends far fewer pointer moves than his screen: anything smoothed over moves (direction, pressure) must be smoothed over distance, never per move.
- Anything read from the finger's speed must be tested with moves that carry their own times (flick in flows.py, CDP timestamps): the test browser sends few, late moves, so wall-clock drags give it wrong speeds.
- A stroke's state that changes with speed (lifting) changes evenly along each move, dab by dab; set once per move, a long move paints all of it with the end value.
- The test browser reads back black past the paper's edge: anything that paints test strokes (previews, looks) must keep them on the paper.
- Random marks (spray drops, ink hairs) differ every stroke: a test on them measures many places or a band, never one row.
- Ink splash drops fly far (about 4 brush widths): keep them off other test rows (splash-0).
- A check piped through cut always exits 0: commit only after grepping the results for FAIL.
- Every step along a stroke needs a floor: a step size that shrinks to 0 when the brush lifts off loops forever and hangs the page (shapes did).
- When a size or spacing grows with speed (shapes), check it against lifting: the flick flow needs the mark narrower half-way (shapes stop growing at SHAPE_TOP_V).
- The full flow run is long (about 30 minutes): run it in the background with PYTHONUNBUFFERED=1 and a long timeout, never in the foreground.
- water_speed can fail when the one-spot frame lands on 17 ms (headless frames come in 16.7 ms steps, so the ratio jumps): rerun it once before looking for a cause.
- Palettes: never make, sample or reshuffle one; offer published 10-colour palettes (Coolors, Lospec, ColorBrewer) as a picture of current vs new, each with why, and change only what he picks.
- A check on spray drops must average a band of rows (new_tools did one row and passed or failed by chance).
- Ink widths differ stroke to stroke by up to about a quarter: a width check on ink averages many places along the line (turn_every), and is still near its limit.
- Pencil's grain contact: an edge term must move the grain value, never one smoothstep edge, or the edges cross near the rim and paint full-colour rings.
- Ink line widths differ stroke to stroke: a width check on ink turns splashes off and averages two lines each way (turn_every).
- A width check along a line judges the width averaged over half a brush: the oil pastel's edge wanders a few pixels at random, and a raw step-to-step check fails by chance (jerk_tail).
- Tests: the whole-game run steps reset by Esc, Basic palette and New; a step that changes the screen size or the tool (stickers hide the buckets) leaves it as he started, or the next steps measure the wrong screen.
- Anything that dries or decays inside a dab must go by time since it was last counted (reset the clock after counting), never be applied again at every dab: wet acrylic dried within one pass and could not be pushed.
- tools/previews.py writes lib/previews but the game file is built from them only by build.py: build again after it, before committing.
- The brush's direction, like pressure, must change dab by dab along each finger move; set once per move, all its dabs share one angle and curves show angled bristle lines.
- Anything that moves paint (the bristles' plough) must move a share of what is there, never subtract a fixed amount: thickness went below zero and left a bare line along strokes.
- Judge a look only beside his reference pictures, at the same scale and close up, and on his own cases (long stroke until empty, scribbles, strokes over wet and dry paint); number tests passed while every brush looked wrong (25 Nov).
- The Mixbox table's alpha is not part of it: read its RGB bytes raw; through a 2D canvas the colours are spoiled where alpha is low and blue + yellow came out grey.
- A brush that picks up wet paint must not pick up its own fresh paint, and can hold only so much: otherwise its paint grows without end.
- A brush is an axis, not an arrow: going back the same way must not flip which bristle is on which side, or back-and-forth strokes bend the paint.
- A bristle's emptying goes per pixel of travel, not per pixel touched: rim bristles touch fewer pixels per dab, kept their paint and left outlines.
- Acrylic references are only owner-shots/2026-11-25-acrylic-1..4 and -blue-strokes; error screenshots from earlier chats are not references. Compare every version with all of them (trials/look_all_refs.py) before sending.
- trials/acrylic-trial.src.html puts several statements on one line: a // comment anywhere but the line's end swallows the code after it (broke the page three times); build_trial.py now refuses a script that does not parse.
