# Tower Builder: cues (voice, visuals, teaching)

> Written from `cues.json` by `tools/cues_doc.py`. Do not edit this file: the owner comments, the table (`cues.json`) changes, this file is written again.

The child plays without reading. The game talks to them, shows them what to do with a see-through hand, and makes things glow. The first site teaches everything step by step. Each later site shows its new way of building in a short demo. After that the child plays alone, and the game only adds flavour: stories, facts and the odd remark.

## How cues work

- A cue: something happens in the game, and the game answers with a voice line, a glow, the ghost hand, a pause, or a mix. Teaching cues explain a rule or a control; flavour cues are stories, praise, facts and remarks.
- Voice: one line at a time, in the order fired. A line lasts its recording (sounds/voice/NN.mp3), or 2 s as a text box whose blue bar runs out (ESC ends it early).
- Every line belongs to the screen it was fired on (the map, one visit to a site, free play); leaving drops what is left.
- Kinds: teach and queued lines wait their turn; bg lines play only if nothing is playing or waiting. When a teaching and a flavour line fire at the same moment, the flavour one is dropped.
- No line starts while a test runs unless it says it may (during: test).
- While a teaching line plays or waits its turn, or a step waits for the child, every other line is dropped (a dropped first-time line keeps its first time for later). In the free building between steps (the child building on his own while the next step waits for enough pieces) other lines play as usual (owner 15 Nov night: the first crumble line must still play on the first site).
- Lines about a moment of building (a crumble, a snap) are not said during a test; the test's own lines say what happened.
- An action that ends a waiting step fires nothing else (pressing the red button in the red-button step says no line 70).
- Attention: none (default) only makes what it points at glow; point darkens the rest of the screen while the step waits, and only what it points at (or its touch list) can be touched; freeze darkens and freezes the game while its line plays. A demo darkens all but the building and takes no touch. Ctrl held (parent) is never held back.
- What a line talks about glows from its own outline (a plank's glow is long), with a slow ring spreading out: yellow, or green/red for a green/red thing.
- A step that waits says its line and shows the ghost hand again 5 s after its line ends, until the child does it.
- A step that needs something (a piece in the building) goes back to the step that makes it when that thing is gone (it broke, or the game was reloaded).
- Groups: random, never one of the last n played, n = half the group rounded down (a group of 2 alternates; a site's 3 facts never repeat the last one).
- Memory: one saved record keeps every first time (ever, per site), each group's recent lines and where each teaching sequence stands. The restart clears it, so all teaching, demos and the first welcome come again.
- Every button shows pressed while held and acts when let go over it.
- Wooden supports and forms are taken out by hand (dragged out, or three quick taps send them back). The test does not take them out; one left in shows red. Demos take them out with three taps.
- The test button is red until the building is ready, then green with a 'go' triangle; open sites are yellow, damaged ones red.
- The parent override (Ctrl) has no voice line.
- The map: the open site glows every 60 s (no voice); its voice line plays the first time it opens and every time the child comes back from the site before it, 2 s after the win line ends. A flavour line plays every 30 s of quiet.
- The restart: the first press says what it does; a second press while its line lasts erases everything.
- A cue is one row: its line (voice) and what it shows (glow, pointing, ghost hand, darkening) start and stop together.
- What the child does wins (owner 17 Nov): when an event the child causes (byChild in Events) fires a cue, whatever cue is playing stops, line and visuals, lines waiting their turn are dropped (their first times stay unused), and the new one starts at once. Not while a teaching line plays or a step waits for the child (the rule above): the first site's teaching is never cut.
- Teaching (the ΔΙΔΑΣΚΑΛΙΑ tick) is exactly the cues that show the child a thing of the game (owner 17 Nov late: "the teaching cues are ONLY those and ALL those that show the game elements"), marked teaching in the table: the first site's steps, "press here to start", the openings, the dots, the shovel, the wooden form, the test's parts and its red, the first win and "press here to go back to the map". Unticked, they are silent (with their glows and hand); every other line plays.

## Demos

On every visit to a site until it is finished, after its welcome line. **tell N**: line N plays while the next pieces are built. **say N**: the demo waits, the game frozen, while line N plays (what came of it). **put ...**: the ghost hand carries these see-through pieces in, at a hand's speed: a number is that piece of the site's suggested build, a list is [shape, material, x, y, angle]. **wait S**: the demo waits S seconds (things settle, crumble or fall). **pull**: the ghost finger taps each wooden form or support three times and it goes back to the pile. **taps SHAPE**: the ghost finger taps that piece three times and it goes back. **fade**: the see-through pieces fade away. **ready**: pieces that already stand when the demo starts (no hand carries them). **put**:  Each comes in over everything and goes straight down onto its place, so it never passes through another piece..

## Words

### Events

- `map-start`: the map shows at the start of the game, or after the restart *(MAIN LOOP start; restart confirm (MAP))*
- `map-shown`: the map shows (start, or coming back from a site) *(MAIN LOOP start; goMap)*
- `welcomed-first`: the first welcome line has been said *(cue then)*
- `tap-locked-site`: the child presses a locked site *(screenButtons (POINTER HANDLING))*
- `press-restart`: the child presses the restart button *(screenButtons)*
- `damage-flash`: every 60 s on the map while a site is damaged (its red rings flash) *(CUES mapTick)*
- `map-return`: the map shows after leaving a won site *(goMap)*
- `map-win-said`: the line about the won site has been said *(cue then)*
- `leave-test`: the child goes to the map in the middle of a test, before a pass *(goMap)*
- `enter-site`: the child enters a site from the map *(screenButtons (a site))*
- `intro-done`: the site's welcome line has been said *(cue then)*
- `plan-shown`: the site is ready for building (after its welcome, teaching or demo) *(cue then; teaching end)*
- `pickup`: the child picks up a piece *(startHold, startPileHold)*
- `tap3`: three quick taps send a piece back to the pile *(onPointerUp)*
- `press-broom`: the child presses the shovel *(pressButton)*
- `press-test`: the child presses the test button (red or green) *(startTest)*
- `press-red`: the child presses the red test button (building not ready) *(startTest)*
- `crumble`: a mudbrick crumbles *(crumble (BREAKING))*
- `snap`: a wooden piece snaps *(snapWood (BREAKING))*
- `pile-empty-red`: the pile is empty and the button is still red *(CUES buildTick)*
- `form-only-left`: everything is ready except a wooden form or support still standing *(CUES buildTick)*
- `test-start`: the test starts (green button) *(startTest)*
- `test-part`: a part of the test begins: wind, load or earthquake *(enterPart (LEVELS))*
- `test-fail`: the test fails in a part *(checkpoint (LEVELS))*
- `fail-explain`: the first fail line of a part has been said *(cue then)*
- `test-pass`: the test passes: the site is won *(testTick (LEVELS))*
- `won`: the win line has been said *(cue then)*
- `first-win-said`: the first-win line has been said *(cue then)*
- `go-map`: the child goes back to the map *(goMap)*
- `idle`: on the map: 30 s with no line *(CUES mapTick)*
- `step`: a teaching step's turn comes (sequences only) *(the sequence runner)*
- `map-next`: coming back to the map from a won site: 2 s after its win line ends, or at once when there is none *(onGoMap)*
- `next-flash`: every 60 s on the map while a site is open and not built *(CUES mapTick)*
- `drag-back`: the child carries a piece from the building into the pile *(worldToPile, onPointerUp)*
- `placed-in-plan`: the child lets go of a piece over the building *(releaseHold)*

### Conditions

- `first-launch`: no saved progress and the first welcome never said
- `site-finished`: the site is won and not damaged
- `site-won-before`: the site was won once (standing or damaged since)
- `teaching-pending`: the site has teaching steps the child has not finished
- `demo-pending`: the site has a demo and is not finished
- `has-openings`: the plan has an opening (door, arch, space between columns)
- `has-damaged`: a site on the map is damaged (red)
- `all-done`: all ten sites are finished
- `opened-next`: a site is open and not built, and the child just left the site before it
- `material:mudbrick`: the piece is mudbrick
- `material:stone`: the piece is stone
- `material:wood`: the piece is wood
- `part:wind`: the part is the wind
- `part:load`: the part is the load
- `part:quake`: the part is the earthquake
- `plan-has-piece`: a piece of the child's is in the building area
- `test-ready`: the test button is green
- `no-form`: no wooden form or support is left in the building
- `visitors-walking`: visitors are walking to a site on the map
- `recorded`: the line has a recording
- `done:<cue>`: that cue has already played
- `not <condition>`: the condition does not hold
- `has-next`: a site is open and not built
- `testing`: a test is running
- `piece-resting`: a piece of the child's lies still in the building area
- `why:headpiece`: the test failed only because the headpiece did not sit well (nothing broke, nothing fell)
- `on-map`: the map is showing
- `first-openings-site`: this is the first site (in map order) whose outline has openings
- `placed:10`: ten or more of the child's pieces stand in the building
- `load-covered`: a piece lies inside the dotted outline
- `placed:3`: three or more of the child's pieces stand in the building
- `placed:8`: eight or more of the child's pieces stand in the building

### Fields

- `on`: the event that fires it
- `if`: conditions that must all hold
- `once`: ever / site (once at each site) / visit (once per visit); none: every time
- `teaching`: true: a teaching cue, one that shows the child a thing of the game (a button, the plan's dots, an opening, the shovel, the test's parts, the red); silent, with its glows and hand, when ΔΙΔΑΣΚΑΛΙΑ is unticked
- `kind`: teach: always plays, waits its turn; queued (default): waits its turn; bg: only if nothing is playing or waiting
- `say`: a line number; or bySite (one per site, in site order); or a group (random, never one of the last half played)
- `attention`: none (default): only what it points at glows; point: the screen darkens except what it points at, only that can be touched, while it waits; freeze: the screen darkens and the game freezes while its line plays
- `points`: what it talks about: each glows from its outline, yellow, or green/red for a green/red thing
- `touch`: what may be touched meanwhile (default: what it points at)
- `show`: the ghost hand acts this out (tap, tap3, drag A > B)
- `wait`: the action it waits for (an event, or until:<condition>); the line and the hand come again 5 s after the line ends
- `needs`: a step that cannot be done without this goes back to the step named in back
- `start`: a step waits for this before it begins
- `after`: a line said when the step is done
- `then`: the event it fires when done
- `do`: teaching: run the site's teaching steps; demo: play the site's demo
- `during`: test: may play while a test runs
- `delay`: waits for that sound to end first
- `glow`: no line: what it points at glows this many seconds
